the stated scope; **red** = measured failure; **amber** = evidence missing.

## Standing honesty notes

- H0-mvp is supported **within its declared scope only**: simulated
  backends, synthetic data-transform families, single machine. It is NOT
  established for real models or broader domains (Phase 5).
- Simulated backends demonstrate mechanism, not model-independence (see
  RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md constraints).
- Test-passing ≠ formal correctness; verification evidence always carries
  explicit claim scopes.
